Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

No account, no cloud sync, no upgrade nags. What you write stays on your computer.

On the desktop, out of the way

Notes are part of the desktop: no taskbar entry, they never cover other windows, and Win+D does not sweep them away.

Click and type, saved as you go

Click the body to start editing — there is no Save button. The title owns its own line and stays put while the body scrolls.

Tick it off, get reminded

Items start with a hollow square — click it while editing to tick it off. Give one a due time (Ctrl+Shift+T) and a countdown badge appears: 2 d left, 3 h left, overdue.

Images, links and folders welcome

Paste a screenshot straight in. Click a URL to open the browser, click a local path to open that folder in Explorer.

Search across everything

Search every note, hidden ones included. Enter jumps straight to the matching line instead of flipping through notes.

Move to a new PC intact

Migrate notes packs the app and your notes into one zip; unzip and double-click on the new machine. Share with friends ships sample notes only — never your own text.

Seven palettes

Yellow, green, pink, purple, blue, gray, charcoal — each note picks its own, so priorities read at a glance.

Everything in the tray

New note, search, show/hide all, start with Windows, Excel export and import — all in the tray menu, no toolbar on your desktop.

A closer look

Just a few pieces, all on the desktop

Right of each title bar is “⋯”; hover it and it expands: new, hide this note, show/hide completed, colour, delete.

FAQ

Things you may want to know first

Where are my notes stored? Is anything uploaded?

In stickydesk.db (a SQLite file) next to the app. No uploads, no account. Delete the folder and it is gone for good.

Then why does it go online at all?

Network access is used only to fetch ad data: the small title-bar ad slot periodically fetches copy from a public promotion list (falling back to a local cache). Your note content never needs the network.

Can I take my notes to another PC?

Yes — tray → Migrate notes packs the app and your notes into one zip; unzip and double-click on the new machine. To pass the app to a friend, use Share with friends: that package only contains sample notes.

macOS or mobile?

Windows only for now. Attaching notes to the desktop layer relies on Windows' desktop window mechanism, so other systems would need a different approach.

Does it cost anything? Is there a paid tier?

It is free: no membership, no stripped-down features, no login wall.
